Why this book

Big brands weren’t built with big money. They were built with clear thinking.

Most branding advice assumes you can hire an agency, run ads and wait. Small business owners can’t. They need decisions that work this week, on this budget, with this team.

Small Money, Big Brand treats your business like a house. Lay the foundation, run the wiring, set the floor plan, open the doors, and build something that keeps standing after the launch buzz fades.

  • Turn a vague problem into a clear next move with a simple think, solve, act rhythm.
  • Find what your customers actually value before you spend on reaching them.
  • Decide which work to automate, which to delegate and which to keep for yourself.
  • Give every person on your team a role they can explain in one sentence.
  • Show up consistently so people recognise, trust and recommend you.
  • Keep the customers you win and build a brand that lasts past you.

About the author

George Ogunbande

George is the Founder and CEO of FlatPurse, an Edmonton company that builds booking and retention software for service businesses. He is a human resources professional with a BA in Labour Studies and HR Management from the University of Windsor, a People Analytics certificate from Wharton, and Associate Membership in CPHR Alberta.

He wrote Small Money, Big Brand for the founder he was at the start: long on ideas, short on cash, and looking for a plan that didn’t assume a marketing department.

Questions

Before you buy.

Is this a branding book?

It started as one. It now reads as an operating manual for founders: brand is the result of how you think, hire, systemise and show up, so the book covers all of it.

Do I need a business already?

No. It works for people at the idea stage and for owners who have been running for years and want more structure.
