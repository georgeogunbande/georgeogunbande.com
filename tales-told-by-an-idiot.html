Why read about mistakes

Success stories tell you what worked once. Screwups tell you what to avoid every time.

Business books are full of winners explaining their wins. The useful part, the wrong turns and the money that disappeared, usually gets cut.

Tales Told by an Idiot keeps that part. Some tales come from George’s own ventures and consulting work. Some are dramatised composites of mistakes he has watched founders make again and again. All of them end the same way: a plain, numbered Idiot Rule you can apply on Monday.

The idiot in the title is usually the author.

About the author

George Ogunbande

George is the Founder and CEO of FlatPurse, an Edmonton company that builds booking and retention software for service businesses. He is a human resources professional with a BA in Labour Studies and HR Management from the University of Windsor, a People Analytics certificate from Wharton, and Associate Membership in CPHR Alberta.

He has started companies, hired the wrong people, run the wrong campaigns and learned something from each one. Tales Told by an Idiot is the book he wishes someone had handed him first.
